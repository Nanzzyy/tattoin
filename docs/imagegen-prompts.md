# Image generation prompts

These project-bound assets were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool in `photorealistic-natural` mode. All prompts required realistic anatomy, tasteful professional tattoo presentation, authentic skin and ink texture, and no blood, nudity, logos, readable text, or watermark.

## Hero — `public/images/hero-studio.png`

Premium tattoo studio landing-page hero: cinematic editorial photograph inside a refined contemporary tattoo studio; a professional artist in black nitrile gloves carefully working on a client's upper arm. Dark charcoal studio, warm practical lights, antique brass detail, faint haze. Gloved hands, tattoo machine, and fresh fine-line botanical tattoo in focus. Wide 16:9 framing with subjects on the right and generous negative space on the left. Dramatic soft side light, deep blacks, restrained crimson glow; realistic anatomy and hands.

## Raven — `public/images/portfolio-raven.png`

Portrait editorial close-up of an elegant healed blackwork raven tattoo with fine botanical and thorn details on an upper arm. Shadowy premium studio, neutral dark background, authentic skin pores, crisp ink, believable arm anatomy, 85mm shallow-depth-of-field look, sculpted soft light, museum-like mood.

## Floral — `public/images/portfolio-floral.png`

Portrait editorial close-up of a delicate single-needle peony and wildflower tattoo wrapping naturally around a shoulder blade. Minimal charcoal studio, authentic skin texture, graceful placement, subtle film grain and soft window-like rim light. Anonymous, tasteful crop with no face.

## Koi — `public/images/portfolio-koi.png`

Landscape editorial close-up of a healed Japanese-inspired koi and wave forearm tattoo. Richly detailed black and muted-crimson composition, blurred premium studio workbench, believable anatomy, tactile ink and skin, directional warm light, restrained saturation.

## Geometry — `public/images/portfolio-geometry.png`

Portrait fashion-editorial photograph of a precise geometric ornamental and dotwork tattoo flowing along a calf. Raw charcoal plaster wall, dark floor, knee-to-ankle crop, narrow softbox side light, architectural mood, realistic healed tattoo and clean leg anatomy.
